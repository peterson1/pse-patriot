## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 5.30% = 10.60%`
- Adjustments applied: **+0.5 for very thin liquidity**
- Reason: turnover is micro (≈₱340k/day average, many days <₱150k) and one PSE tick is
  ~1.08% of price, so quoted moves overshoot fair value; a stop hugging the raw 2×ATR line
  risks being picked off by a single thin print. This is the structural (liquidity/spread)
  widening the methodology allows, not a posture change. `10.60 + 0.5 = 11.1`. Within the
  2–12.5% soft bounds.
- Rounding: `11.1% → ` **`11.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 11.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 11.0 ÷ 5 = 2.20%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 2.5% for tick size + thin book**
- Reason: at ₱0.93 (₱0.50–4.99 band) one tick = ₱0.01 ≈ **1.08%** of price, and the
  bid–ask spread on this name is routinely 1–2 ticks (≈1–2%). A 2.20% buffer is only ≈2.0
  ticks — marginal against tick + spread — so it is widened to the next 0.25 step. 2.5% ≈
  ₱0.023 ≈ 2.3 ticks, clearing 1 tick + a 1-tick spread with margin.
- PSE tick check: buffer ≥ 1 tick + typical spread? **Yes** (2.5% = ₱0.023 vs tick+spread ≈ ₱0.02)
- Rounding: already on a 0.25 step → **`2.5%`**
- **Limit Price = 2.5%**
