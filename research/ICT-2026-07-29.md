## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 3.27% = 6.55%`
- Adjustments applied: **none**
- Reason for each adjustment: no widening is warranted — ICT is one of the deepest books on the
  PSE at ≈ ₱1.63B/day, so there is no thin-name or spread penalty to pay for. Nor is there a
  gappiness case: across all 14 sessions every daily range overlapped the previous close (the
  largest TR, ₱51 on 21-Jul, was an intraday reversal, not an overnight gap), so the plain
  High−Low volatility the ATR already measures is the whole risk. Baseline stands.
- Rounding: `6.55% → ` **`6.5%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 6.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 6.5 ÷ 5 = 1.3%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** (1.3% rounds down to the nearest 0.25 → 1.25%)
- Reason for each adjustment: at ₱1.63B of daily turnover the book absorbs size at a tick or two;
  the baseline buffer is already ~25× the tick, so neither widening nor further tightening is
  justified. The buffer is left at the formula value rather than pushed toward the 0.25–0.5%
  blue-chip floor because ICT's *daily range* is genuinely large (₱13–51) — deep liquidity makes
  the fill likely, but a fast ₱40 down-day still needs real room below the trigger.
- PSE tick check: at ₱982.00 (₱500.00–₱999.50 band) one tick = **₱0.50 ≈ 0.051%**. Buffer 1.25% =
  ₱12.28 ≈ **24.6 ticks**, far above 1 tick + typical spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.3% → ` **`1.25%`**
- **Limit Price = 1.25%**
