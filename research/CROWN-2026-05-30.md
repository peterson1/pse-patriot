## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.80% = 3.59%`
- Adjustments applied: **+~1.4 pts for extreme thin liquidity / thin-book gap & spread risk.**
- Reason: Turnover is only ~₱191k/day (volume as low as 1,000 shares on 15-May) — one of the
  thinnest names in the dataset. A pure 3.6% stop on a thin book is easily picked off by a
  single-tick spread blip or a gap to the range floor (the name tagged ₱1.640 on both 11-May
  and 18-May). Widening gives the trade room to survive a range-low tag while still exiting on
  a genuine breakdown. The widening is held *moderate* (not extreme) because realized
  volatility is genuinely low — the stock sat in a ~4.7% band (₱1.640–1.720) for the whole
  3-week window.
- Rounding: `~5.0% →` **`5.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 5.0%**

Sanity check vs. price structure: from a ₱1.720 high, a 5.0% offset triggers at ≈ ₱1.634 —
just below the ₱1.640 range floor, so it survives a tag of the established range low but fires
on a true break. (The prior 8.5% would have triggered at ≈ ₱1.574, below the 52-week low of
₱1.600 — wider than the stock's entire annual range, effectively never arming for a
tight-posture trader.)

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 5.0 ÷ 5 = 1.0%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 1.5%** for thin-book / wide-spread fill risk.
- Reason: On a name this illiquid the stop-limit must clear at least one tick plus the typical
  spread or it may sit inside the spread and never fill in a fast drop. The 1.0% baseline
  (≈ ₱0.017) is barely above a single tick and below tick + spread.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.710 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.585%) —
  empirically confirmed by the data, which prints only in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 1.5% ≈ ₱0.0257
  ≈ **2.5 ticks**, comfortably exceeding 1 tick + a typical 1-tick thin-book spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.5% →` **`1.5%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
