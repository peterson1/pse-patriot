## Recommended OCO bottom-leg (trailing stop) values

| Parameter | Recommended | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Trailing Offset** | **7.0%** | ≈ 2× the 14-day ATR (3.51%); also clears the observed −5.95% single-session intraday air-pocket (20-May) with margin so the stop is not wicked out by routine pullbacks. |
| **Limit Price (execution buffer)** | **1.5%** | At ~36.70 PHP, the PSE tick is 0.05 (~0.14%); 1.5% ≈ 0.55 PHP ≈ 11 ticks. Given only moderate (recently light) liquidity and demonstrated ~6% intraday plunges, execution certainty on the protective leg outweighs a marginally better fill. |

**Why not tighter?** A 5–6% trail would have been triggered by the 20-May intraday wick; recent intraday
downside still reaches ~6%, so a sub-6.5% offset risks premature stop-outs. **Why not wider?** Beyond ~8%
gives back too much profit on a short-term swing. 7% / 1.5% is the balanced choice for current conditions.
