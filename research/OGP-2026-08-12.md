## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.7557% = 5.5113%`
- Adjustments applied: **+1.28% for the dated 19-Aug-2026 ex-dividend** (structural event risk)
- Reason for each adjustment:
  - **Ex-dividend, 19-Aug-2026 (₱0.46 ≈ 1.28% of ₱35.90).** This is a *mechanical* mark-down on
    no news — precisely what a trailing stop must not fire on. Left at the 5.51% baseline, the
    noise budget on the ex-date collapses to ≈4.23%, i.e. only **1.5× ATR** — materially tighter
    than the intended two-ATR-day posture. Adding the dividend back preserves the posture *through*
    the event: `5.5113% + 1.2813% = 6.7927%`.
  - **Gappiness has returned** (supporting, not double-counted). The 29-Jul review explicitly held
    at baseline because the gap-prone tape had rolled out. It is back: the 6-Aug reversal printed a
    **₱2.30 TR = 6.4% of price**, and overnight gap-ups of +2.15% / +2.47% / +2.61% recurred. A
    7.0% offset also clears that worst-TR bar, which 5.5% does not.
  - **Liquidity: no widening.** Turnover *improved* from ≈₱18.2M/day (29-Jul) to ≈₱30.1M/day, so
    the thin-name argument does not apply and was not used.
  - **Counterweight, applied:** one event day carries ~10% of the measured ATR — excluding 6-Aug
    the mean TR is ₱0.888 (2.47%), so 2.76% is already slightly inflated. This is why the offset
    was **not** pushed to the 2.5× ATR (≈6.9%) that the volatility alone might suggest; the 7.0%
    is derived from baseline + dividend, and the two happen to land in the same place.
- Rounding: `6.7927% → ` **`7.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 7.0%**

> **If the position is re-cut after the 19-Aug ex-date, the dividend component drops out and the
> correct offset reverts to ≈5.5%.** The extra 1.5 points is explicitly rented for one event.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 7.0 ÷ 5 = 1.40%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** beyond rounding
- Reason for each adjustment: turnover of ≈₱30.1M/day is adequate depth for a ~1.5% buffer on a
  ₱35.90 stock, so no illiquidity widening is required. Two facts argued *for* widening further
  (to offset ÷ 4 ≈ 1.75%) and were considered and rejected: the live bid–ask was **never measured**
  (every L1 source — Investagrams, pesobility, PSE Edge quote board — failed or 403'd), and
  adverse overnight gaps run 2–3%. They were rejected because 1.50% already equals **10.8 ticks**,
  comfortably beyond any plausible 1–3 tick spread on a ₱30M/day book. The unmeasured spread is
  recorded here as the weakest input in this note.
- PSE tick check: at ₱35.90 (₱20.00–₱49.95 band) one tick = **₱0.05 ≈ 0.1393%**. Buffer 1.50% =
  **₱0.5385 ≈ 10.8 ticks**, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.40% → ` **`1.50%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.50%**

Narrative: since 29-Jul-2026 OGP **broke out of the ₱32.50–33.00 consolidation and ran +10.3%**
(₱32.55 → ₱35.90), part of a +15.8% month off the 13-Jul close of ₱31.00. Crucially, the ATR
barely moved (2.74% → 2.76%) — but its *composition* changed. The 29-Jul window was a quiet grind
