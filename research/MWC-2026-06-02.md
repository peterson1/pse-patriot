## 3. Trailing Offset computation

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 6.5 ÷ 5 = 1.30%`
- Adjustments applied: none (1.30% → 1.25% is just rounding).
- Reason: MWC's only moderate liquidity and the current fast, one-way drops argue for keeping
  the buffer at the formula level rather than tightening; this ensures the stop-limit still
  fills in a sharp move down.
- PSE tick check: at ₱39.05 (band ₱20.00–₱49.99) one tick = ₱0.05 (0.13%); buffer 1.25% =

- Rounding: `1.30% →` **`1.25%`** (nearest 0.25)
