## 2. 14-day ATR

**No published ATR was usable.** Exactly one vendor publishes one — investing.com, ATR(14) = ₱0.0129 (20-Jul-2026 05:50 GMT, method undisclosed) — and it is rejected:

- **It fails an arithmetic diagnostic.** Against the OHLC series two vendors agree on to the share, the mean of `High − Low` is ₱0.0136 and the mean gap-inclusive True Range is ₱0.0243. The published ₱0.0129 sits essentially on the high-low-only figure and nowhere near the true-range figure — **it appears to drop the gap term.** On this specific name that is not a rounding quibble: gaps are the *entire* risk, and intraday range is the component that barely exists.
- **The page carrying it is broken.** It tags the stock "High Volatility" while publishing a 0.73%-of-price ATR, and reports ADX(14) = 80.512 labelled "Oversold" — a category error (ADX is non-directional and cannot be oversold).
- **Single point of failure.** TradingView's technicals render every field as "—", Barchart 404s on `CROWN.PH`, stockanalysis publishes no ATR, Investagrams requires login. Nothing can check it.

A gathering agent used ₱0.0129 to conclude the ATR% downtrend *continues* (0.85% → 0.73%) and self-rated high confidence. **That conclusion is rejected** — the transcription was accurate, the framing was not.

- **Sum of 14 True Ranges:** ₱0.34
- **14-day ATR = 0.34 ÷ 14 =** **₱0.0243**
- **Current price:** ₱1.770 (last settled close, 17-Jul-2026)
- **ATR% = 0.0243 ÷ 1.770 × 100 =** **1.37%**

### The mechanism matters more than the level

Of the 14 True Ranges, **only one (07-Jul) is set by intraday range alone**; four are set *purely* by the gap term on sessions with zero intraday range. The starkest is **14-Jul**: the largest TR in the window (₱0.04, 2.3% of price) on a day when high == low == ₱1.750 and no intraday movement occurred at all — the price simply reappeared four ticks below the prior close.

The two possible ATR measures diverge by ~1.8×, and the choice is decision-relevant:

| Measure | ATR | % of price |
|---|---|---|
| Gap-inclusive True Range (**used here**) | ₱0.0243 | **1.37%** |
| High − Low only | ₱0.0136 | 0.77% |

On a tape where 7 of 20 sessions print high == low but closes jump 3–4 ticks overnight, the high-low measure understates real risk by nearly half. **A stop on this name is jumped over, not walked through.**

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.37% = 2.74%`
- Adjustments applied: **+2.26 pts for thin-book gap and spread risk** → 5.0%
- Reason for each adjustment:
  1. **Turnover has deteriorated sharply — this is the dominant change.** Median session turnover is now **₱93,270/day** (mean ₱174,410; ex-block mean ₱105,481), against ~₱230,000–285,000/day in the prior note — roughly **36% of what that note assumed**. **10 of the last 20 sessions traded under 50,000 shares**; six under 25,000; the thinnest was 13-Jul at **6,000 shares** (six board lots, ~₱10,740 of turnover *for the entire day*). An exit of even 50,000 shares (~₱88,000) would be a large fraction of, or exceed, a median day's entire volume.
  2. **Volatility is now gap-shaped.** 7 of 20 sessions had *no intraday price discovery whatsoever*. Four ran consecutively (13–16 Jul) with closes 1.79 → 1.75 → 1.75 → 1.78 — the price moved four ticks down then three up **entirely through overnight gaps**, on 6,000–46,000 shares/day. A stop cannot be walked to; it is gapped through.
  3. **Tick coarseness.** One tick is 0.565% of price and the ATR is 2.43 ticks, so any stop tighter than ~1.2% sits inside two ticks of ordinary noise.
- **Deliberately *not* double-counted:** the gap risk is already inside the gap-inclusive ATR, which is precisely why ATR% expanded. The premium here is sized for the *liquidity* deterioration and spread uncertainty on top of that, not for gappiness a second time — which is why it lands at ~2.26 pts rather than being stacked higher.
- Independent structural check: `2 × worst recent gap (₱0.04) + 1 tick (₱0.01) = ₱0.09 = 5.08%` — converges on the same answer from a different direction.
- Event risk contributed **nothing** to this widening (see §5); the case rests entirely on gap and liquidity mechanics.
- Soft bounds: 5.0% sits comfortably inside the ~2%–12.5% band. No floor or ceiling hit.
- Rounding: `4.99% → ` **`5.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 5.0%**

**Sanity check vs price structure.** From the recent ₱1.790 high-water mark, a 5.0% offset triggers at **≈ ₱1.7005**. The 20-session close box is ₱1.740–1.790, so the stop sits five ticks below the box floor — it survives the current range, and it fires almost exactly on the **₱1.700 early-June floor**, i.e. on a genuine break of the base. For comparison, the outgoing 4.0% would trigger at ₱1.7184 — only ~2 ticks below the box low, and barely more than one 14-Jul-style pure gap (₱0.04) of cushion.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 5.0 ÷ 5 = 1.00%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 1.75%**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  1. **The baseline is barely more than one tick.** 1.00% = ₱0.0177 ≈ **1.77 ticks**. If the spread is even one tick, that buffer sits essentially inside it.
  2. **The spread is unverified and could be up to ~3 ticks.** No accessible source publishes Level-1 quotes, depth, or a spread statistic for PSE:CROWN — not PSE Edge's public page, not stockanalysis, not investing.com, not TradingView; Pesobility returns HTTP 403 and Investagrams requires login. The defensible working range is **1–3 ticks (0.57%–1.70%)**, and that is an *inference from the tick grid and volume profile, not an observation*. A 1.5% buffer (2.65 ticks) would sit **inside** a 3-tick spread and might never fill. **1.75% = ₱0.031 ≈ 3.1 ticks** just clears the pessimistic case — which is the case worth sizing for when the number is unmeasured.
  3. **Liquidity got worse since the buffer was last set.** Median turnover under ₱100,000/day, with sessions of 6,000 and 9,000 shares, means a triggered stop can fill materially away from the trigger. On this name the limit buffer is the **critical leg**.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.770 the price sits in the ₱0.50–4.99 band → tick **₱0.010** (0.565%), board lot **1,000** — confirmed against the PSE Academy board-lot table *and* independently against PSE Edge's own page. All 25 observed sessions print exclusively on the ₱0.010 grid, empirically confirming it. Buffer 1.75% ≈ **3.1 ticks** ≥ 1 tick + a 2-tick spread. ✅
- Ratio sanity check: 1.75 ÷ 5.0 = **35% of the offset**, well above the normal 15–20% band. Logged deliberately — the methodology's documented widening for illiquid micro-caps (which "can need 2–3%") applies here about as strongly as anywhere in this file.
- Rounding: `1.00% → ` **`1.75%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.75%**

**Narrative.** Between 30-Jun and 17-Jul-2026 CROWN's *price* went essentially nowhere — ₱1.760 → ₱1.770, +0.57%, inside a five-tick box — but its *microstructure* deteriorated on both axes that matter for a stop. ## 9. Known limitations (read before trusting these values)

1. **The bid-ask spread is unmeasured** — the single most decision-relevant unknown, and the direct reason the buffer was widened to 3.1 ticks. Every "1–3 tick" figure here is inference from the tick grid plus volume, not observation. Four agents independently guessing the same range is **not** corroboration. A real number needs a PSE-connected broker terminal or paid Level-2 data.
2. **The official disclosure feed returned empty.** `edge.pse.com.ph/companyDisclosures/search.ax` for `cmpy_id=657` returned no data on two attempts, so "no pending disclosures" **cannot be proven from the authoritative source** — only from the company's own IR page. This is the main reason confidence is capped at medium.
3. **No historical bar is exchange-official.** PSE Edge publishes only the current session; every ATR row is third-party, mitigated by exact two-vendor agreement plus the PSE Edge current-session anchor.
4. **The prior note's ATR method is unknowable.** It said only "simple 14-day ATR" without defining the range term. If it meant gap-inclusive True Range, the expansion reading above is apples-to-apples. If it meant a high-minus-low mean, the comparable figure today is ₱0.0136 / 0.77% and compression would appear to continue. The two methods differ ~1.8× on this name. **The gap-inclusive measure is the correct one for stop sizing here** (see §2), so that is what was acted on — but the ambiguity is real and is not hidden.
5. **No true 3-month baseline.** Verified bars reach back only to 02-Jun-2026 (and only to 22-Jun on two sources). The 20-session volume average must **not** be read as a 3-month average.
6. **The 20-Jul session is excluded as unsettled.** Its 428,000-share / ₱749,030 bar is double-sourced but provisional — and on a book where two sessions carry 45% of monthly volume, that is precisely the bar not to anchor on. Re-pull after it settles if it matters.
7. **The 30-Jun/01-Jul volume spike is unexplained** — 45% of the 20-session volume, one tick of price movement, no identified cause, and the only proposed explanation rejected as fabricated.
8. **No trade-count or intraday data.** A 6,000-share day could be a single print. Q1-2026 earnings could not be re-verified. No Philippine editorial coverage surfaced since 30-Jun — plausible for a ₱1B micro-cap, but "no news found" ≠ "no news exists".
9. Overall confidence: **medium**, driven mainly by limitations 1 and 2.
