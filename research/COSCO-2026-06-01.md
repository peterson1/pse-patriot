### PSE disambiguation
Confirmed this is **Cosco Capital, Inc.**, the Philippine holding company of the Lucio Co group
(grocery retail via Puregold, specialty retail, liquor distribution, real estate, oil & mining),
listed on the PSE under ticker **COSCO** and quoted in **Philippine pesos (₱)**. This is **not**
COSCO Shipping Holdings (the Chinese state shipping group listed in Hong Kong / Shanghai). Evidence:
the PSE EDGE company page (`edge.pse.com.ph/companyPage/stockData.do?cmpy_id=50`), stockanalysis.com
tags it `PSE:COSCO` in the Philippines consumer-retailing sector, and Bloomberg lists it as
`COSCO:PM` (PM = Philippines). The price magnitude (~₱7, market cap ~₱49B, 5.7% PHP dividend yield)
is consistent with a mid-cap PSE holding company and rules out a wrong-exchange match.
