## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.226% = 2.45%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen ~+0.55% for thin liquidity + gap risk; honor ~2% soft floor.**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  - **Liquidity / spread:** AUB is thin — 3-month average volume ~25,050 sh/day (~₱1.1M/day) and the
    05-Jun session traded only ~1,500 sh / ₱65,575. Several recent sessions printed only a handful of
    lots (e.g. Jun 3 = 2,400 sh, Jun 4 quote-page 3,600 sh). On a name this thin a 2.45% stop sits
    inside ordinary noise/wide spreads and gets picked off; the methodology says nudge the offset up
    for low turnover.
  - **Gappiness / event risk:** May 28 printed a single-day TR of ₱1.35 (range ₱42.65–44.00) on 61,100
    shares — a block/news-driven outlier well above the other ~₱0.4 days. That episodic gap risk argues
    for a touch more room than the pure 2× average suggests.
- Rounding: `~3.0% → ` **`3.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 3.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 3.0 ÷ 5 = 0.60%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen to 0.75% for thin liquidity / wide spread.**
- Reason for each adjustment: With only ~1–3k shares trading on quiet days, the bid–ask spread can be
  several ticks wide. A 0.60% buffer (₱0.26) is workable but a slightly wider 0.75% (₱0.33) gives more
  certainty the stop-limit actually fills on a fast drop without sitting inside the spread. Keeping it at
  0.75 also matches the previous entry's well-fitted execution buffer for this thin name.
- PSE tick check: at ₱43.70 the price falls in the ₱10.00–₱49.99 band, where one PSE tick = ₱0.05
  (0.114%). Buffer 0.75% = ₱0.33 ≈ ~6–7 ticks, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread. **Yes.**
- Rounding: `0.75% → ` **`0.75%`** (nearest 0.25, floor 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
