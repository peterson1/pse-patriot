## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 5.25% = 10.49%`
- Adjustments applied: **none.** ICT is a deep blue chip (~₱1.5–1.7B/day turnover), so no
  liquidity widening is needed; and the elevated ATR% is real swing volatility, not thin-book
  noise, so it is **not** trimmed back toward the floor.
- Reason: deep liquidity means the stop won't be picked off by spread noise, so the 2.0×
  baseline stands as-is. With daily ranges frequently ₱40–82, a tighter stop would be whipsawed
  out on an ordinary session of the current tape.
- Rounding: `10.49% →` **`10.5%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 10.5%**

Sanity check: 10.5% sits inside the ~12.5% soft ceiling. From a ₱890 high-water mark a 10.5%
offset triggers at ≈ ₱796 — below the entire June 9–29 trading range (low ₱851) and around the
early-June launch zone (₱778–830), so it only fires on a full retracement of the June advance,
not on the routine ₱40–80 intraday swings.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 10.5 ÷ 5 = 2.10%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened to 1.5%** for deep liquidity.
- Reason: ICT is one of the most liquid names on the PSE (deep order book, ~₱1.5–1.7B/day
  turnover), so the bid–ask spread is only a tick or two and a full 2.10% buffer is
  unnecessary. It is **not** tightened all the way to the 0.25–0.5% blue-chip band, however,
  because the stock is currently gappy and fast-moving — several recent sessions closed on
  their lows after ₱40–80 intraday drops (e.g. 24-Jun closed ₱851 = low) — so 1.5% keeps
  enough cushion to fill on a fast reversal.
- PSE tick check: at ₱890 the PSE tick is **₱0.50** (₱500.00–999.50 band) ≈ 0.056%; prices
  print in ₱0.50 steps (e.g. 891.50, 913.50, 887.50), confirming the band. The 1.5% buffer
  (₱13.35 ≈ 27 ticks) clears one tick plus the typical spread many times over. ✔
- Rounding: `2.10% →` **`1.5%`** (after liquidity tightening, nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
