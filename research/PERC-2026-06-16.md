## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.66% = 5.32%`
- Adjustments applied: **+~2.7 pts for extreme thin-book gap risk → ~3.0× ATR.**
- Reason: turnover averages only ~₱34k/day (≈9k shares), with sessions as thin as 1,000–6,000
  shares where a single order gaps the price 4–5%. The observed thin-book down-moves (₱3.78→₱3.62
  on 29-May, ₱3.76→₱3.59 on 3-Jun) are ~4.5%; a pure 5.3% stop barely clears them and would
  whipsaw on ordinary thin-book noise. Widening to ~3.0× ATR gives the trade room to survive
  that noise while still exiting on a genuine breakdown. Held below the prior 11% (see §5) and
  inside the ~2–12.5% soft band.
- Rounding: `~8.0% →` **`8.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 8.0%**
- Sanity check: from the ₱3.730 high-water mark, an 8.0% offset triggers at ≈ ₱3.43 —
  comfortably below the entire recent range (₱3.56–3.90) and the thin-book dip floor (₱3.56–3.60),
  so ordinary thin-book gaps would *not* whipsaw the trade out, yet a true break below the base
  still fires the stop. Largest thin-book gap (~₱0.17) is well inside the ₱0.30 of room.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 8.0 ÷ 5 = 1.60%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 2.0%** for extreme illiquidity / fill risk.
- Reason: when the stop triggers on a 9k-share/day book there may be no buyers near the limit;
  the buffer is the **critical protection** that the exit actually executes. Per methodology,
  illiquid micro-caps warrant 2–3%. 2.0% (= 25% of the offset, above the normal 15–20% band) is
  the deliberate illiquidity widening; it stops short of 2.5–3% because the *realized* range is
  genuinely modest (2.66% ATR) so an over-wide limit would give back too much.
- PSE tick check: at ₱3.730 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.268%) —
  confirmed by the data, which prints in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 2.0% ≈ ₱0.0746 ≈ **7.5 ticks**,
  comfortably exceeding 1 tick + a wide thin-book spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.60% →` **`2.0%`** (widened for illiquidity as above; nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 2.0%**
