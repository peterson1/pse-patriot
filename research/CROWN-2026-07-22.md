## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.30% = 2.6%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen for thin liquidity / gap risk**
- Reason for each adjustment: CROWN is a genuine micro-cap — many sessions trade well under
  ₱100k value, with interspersed dead days and occasional large single prints. The measured
  ATR understates real risk because zero-move days (no trades) pull the average down; a single
  seller can knock the price a tick or two (₱0.01–0.02 ≈ 0.6–1.1%) instantly. Widening ~1.4pp
  above the raw baseline gives a thin-name cushion while still respecting the compressed
  realized volatility.
- Rounding: `2.6% → widen → ` **`4.0%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 4.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.0 ÷ 5 = 0.8%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen for illiquid / wide-spread micro-cap**
- Reason for each adjustment: on a name that sometimes does not trade, the limit must reach
  down far enough to actually fill when a seller finally hits the book. A buffer near the
  0.8% baseline (~1.4 ticks) risks sitting inside the spread on a fast, thin drop.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.76 (₱0.50–₱4.99 band), one tick = ₱0.01 ≈ 0.57%; buffer 1.5% = ₱0.026
  ≈ 2–3 ticks, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread. ✔
- Rounding: `0.8% → widen → ` **`1.5%`**
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
