## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 4.24% = 8.49%`
- Adjustments applied: none for liquidity (ICT is a deep blue chip with billions/day of
  turnover, so no widening needed); the elevated ATR% is genuine rally/news-driven volatility,
  not liquidity noise, so it is left intact rather than trimmed.
- Reason for each adjustment: deep liquidity means the stop won't be picked off by spread noise,
  so the 2.0× baseline stands without a liquidity nudge.
- Rounding: `8.49% → ` **`8.5%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 8.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 8.5 ÷ 5 = 1.70%`
- Adjustments applied: tightened from 1.70% toward 1.25%.
- Reason for each adjustment: ICT is one of the most liquid names on the PSE (deep order book,
  ~₱1.3–2.4B/day turnover), so the bid–ask spread is only a tick or two and a full 1.70% buffer
  is unnecessary. Not tightened all the way to the 0.25–0.5% blue-chip band, however, because
  the stock is currently gappy and fast-moving near a new all-time high (e.g. Jun 11 ranged
  ₱888–935); 1.25% keeps enough cushion to fill on a fast reversal.
- PSE tick check: at ₱898 the PSE tick is ₱0.50 (₱500.00–999.50 band) ≈ 0.056%; the 1.25%
  buffer (₱11.23) clears one tick plus the typical spread many times over. ✔
- Rounding: `1.70% → ` **`1.25%`** (after liquidity tightening, rounded to nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.25%**
