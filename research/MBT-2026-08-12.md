## 2. 14-day ATR

Computed from the reconciled daily OHLC. **Method: simple average of the last 14 True Ranges**
(not Wilder's — this is computed here rather than cited, and the simple form is hand-verifiable).
TR = max(High − Low, |High − PrevClose|, |Low − PrevClose|). All values in ₱. The **22-Jul close of
₱66.55** seeds the first Prev Close; the 14 TRs run 23-Jul → 11-Aug.

| Date | High | Low | Prev Close | True Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jul 23 | 67.00 | 66.30 | 66.55 | 0.70 |
| Jul 24 | 66.55 | 65.25 | 66.50 | 1.30 |
| Jul 27 | 66.95 | 66.20 | 66.20 | 0.75 |
| Jul 28 | 67.40 | 66.60 | 66.60 | 0.80 |
| Jul 29 | 68.20 | 66.95 | 66.95 | 1.25 |
| Jul 30 | 67.80 | 66.50 | 67.50 | 1.30 |
| Jul 31 | 67.40 | 65.70 | 66.50 | **1.70** |
| Aug 3 | 67.00 | 65.70 | 65.70 | 1.30 |
| Aug 4 | 67.15 | 66.15 | 66.20 | 1.00 |
| Aug 5 | 66.80 | 65.90 | 66.20 | 0.90 |
| Aug 6 | 67.35 | 66.20 | 66.65 | 1.15 |
| Aug 7 | 67.00 | 65.70 | 67.00 | 1.30 |
| Aug 10 | 66.50 | 65.95 | 66.20 | 0.55 |
| Aug 11 | 67.50 | 66.20 | 66.30 | 1.30 |

- **Sum of 14 True Ranges:** ₱15.30
- **14-day ATR = sum ÷ 14 =** ₱1.09286
- **Current price:** ₱66.95
- **ATR% = ATR ÷ price × 100 = 1.09286 ÷ 66.95 × 100 =** **1.6323%**

*Note:* three TRs exceed their plain High − Low because of gaps — 24-Jul (₱1.30 = |65.25 − 66.50|,
a gap-down), 03-Aug (₱1.30 = |67.00 − 65.70| after 31-Jul closed on its low) and 07-Aug.

*Convention sensitivity (disclosed):* a **Wilder-smoothed** ATR(14) would give ₱1.0398 = **1.553%**,
4.9% lower, which would put the baseline at 3.11% and round to 3.0%. The simple average is used
per methodology.

*Source sensitivity (disclosed):* filgit.com reports the 11-Aug range as 67.40/66.30 rather than
67.50/66.20, which would cut that day's TR from ₱1.30 to ₱1.10 and the ATR to ₱1.0786 (1.61%).
**The two-source value was adopted** — stockanalysis and Investing.com PH agree, and Investing.com's
*overview* page (a different page from its historical grid) independently states the day's range as
66.20–67.50, making it 3-vs-1. filgit is the outlier and was independently discredited elsewhere
(it also mis-states the 52-week band as 62.05/78.00).

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.6323% = 3.2647%`
- Adjustments applied: **none**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  - **Liquidity: no widening.** ≈₱126.6M/day, and even the thinnest session in the window (11-Aug,
    ₱80.9M) is very deep. The ₱0.05 tick is just 0.075% of price. Nothing here justifies extra room.
    *(The claim that current turnover runs 35–45% below the ₱202M 1-year average comes solely from
    filgit — the one source discredited above — so it is treated as indicative only and was not
    used as a widening argument.)*
  - **Realised volatility: no widening — it arguably argues the other way.** This is an unusually
    gap-free tape: the largest close-to-close move in 22 sessions is −1.48%, the largest overnight
    gap is +1.22%, and there is **not one down-gap in the window**. Trailing-5 mean TR (₱1.04) sits
    *below* the 14-day ATR (₱1.09).
  - **Forward event risk: flagged, deliberately NOT baked in.** See the box below — this is the one
    argument for widening and it was rejected on evidentiary grounds, not because it is unimportant.
- Rounding: `3.2647% → ` **`3.5%`** (nearest 0.5; 3.2647 is 0.2353 from 3.5 vs 0.2647 from 3.0)
- **Trailing Offset = 3.5%**

> ### ⚠ The September dividend — why it is flagged rather than priced in
>
> Metrobank declares its full-year dividend once (18-Feb-2026: regular ₱1.50 + special ₱2.00, paid
> 26-Mar-2026) and pays a **second regular ₱1.50 tranche later in the year**. On two years of
> precedent (record 08-Sep-2025; record 05-Sep-2024) that tranche goes ex in the **first week of
> September 2026**. At ₱66.95 that is a **mechanical 2.24% overnight drop — 1.4× the entire 14-day
> ATR and ~1.8× the largest gap in 22 sessions.** At a 3.5% offset it would consume ~64% of the
> stop distance on its own.
>
> **It is not baked into the offset because the evidence does not support it yet:** the ex-date is
> marked **TBC** by Metrobank IR, is **single-sourced**, was **never checked against PSE Edge's own
> corporate-action feed**, and is **~3–4 weeks out**. Widening now would spend real give-back on a
> date that has not been published.
>
> *(This is the opposite call from the same-day OGP review, deliberately: OGP's ex-date is
> confirmed on two sources, dated 19-Aug-2026, and only five sessions away — so there it **was**
> added to the offset.)*
>
> **Action for the human: re-run this ticker before ~25-Aug-2026 and confirm the ex-date against
> PSE Edge.** If it lands in early September and the position is still open, the offset should widen
> to roughly 2.2–2.5× ATR% (≈4.0–4.5%) to absorb the mark-down. Also note the **BSP Monetary Board
> meets 27-Aug-2026** into a split street — PSE banks fell 1.63% in a single session on the April
> 2026 hike, i.e. a full ATR in one print.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 3.5 ÷ 5 = 0.70%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** beyond rounding
- Reason for each adjustment: MBT is a deep blue chip, which normally argues for *tightening*
  toward the 0.25–0.5% floor used for SM/BDO/SMPH. It was not tightened, because the baseline
  already lands at a sensible ≈10 ticks and the live bid–ask was **never measured** — no source
  published Level-1 depth, so the "1–2 ticks top-of-book" expectation is an inference from the
  ₱0.05 price grid, not an observation. Leaving the buffer at baseline costs little and covers that
  unmeasured spread.
- PSE tick check: at ₱66.95 (₱50.00–₱99.95 band) one tick = **₱0.05 ≈ 0.0747%**. Buffer 0.75% =
  **₱0.502 ≈ 10.0 ticks**, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread. ✔ Every price in the 22-row window
  is an exact multiple of ₱0.05, confirming the band.
- Rounding: `0.70% → ` **`0.75%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
