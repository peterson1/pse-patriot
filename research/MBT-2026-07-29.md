## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.41% = 2.83%`
- Adjustments applied: **none**
- Reason for each adjustment: MBT is a deep, liquid large-cap bank (≈ ₱116M/day) with no
  thin-name or spread penalty to pay. There is no gappiness case either — all 14 TRs sit in a
  tight ₱0.60–1.45 band with no outliers, and every daily range overlapped the prior close, so
  the ATR is capturing the entire risk picture rather than understating gap exposure. The value
  sits comfortably above the ~2% soft floor, so no floor adjustment is needed. Baseline stands.
- Rounding: `2.83% → ` **`3.0%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 3.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 3.0 ÷ 5 = 0.6%`
- Adjustments applied: **modest tighten to 0.5%** toward the blue-chip band (0.6% rounds to the
  nearest 0.25 → 0.5%)
- Reason for each adjustment: at ₱116M/day the book fills at a tick or two, so the deep-liquidity
  tightening in the methodology applies. It is **not** pushed all the way to the 0.25% floor used
  for SM/BDO: MBT's daily range still runs ₱0.60–1.45, so a stop firing on a ₱1.30 down-day needs
  more than a single tick of room below the trigger.
- PSE tick check: at ₱66.95 (₱50.00–₱99.95 band) one tick = **₱0.05 ≈ 0.075%**. Buffer 0.5% =
  ₱0.335 ≈ **6.7 ticks**, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread on a name this liquid. ✔
- Rounding: `0.6% → ` **`0.5%`**
- **Limit Price = 0.5%**
