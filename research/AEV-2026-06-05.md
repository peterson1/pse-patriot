## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.20% = 4.39%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** (no structural widen/tighten).
- Reason: AEV is a liquid PSE large-cap (≈₱33M/day turnover, ~1.05M sh/day, ₱180B cap) with
  tight spreads and orderly daily ranges; no thin-liquidity, gap, or event risk to justify
  widening, and the chosen posture is deliberately tight. The lone larger range day (29-May,
  ₱1.50) is already captured inside the ATR.
- Rounding: `4.39% →` round to nearest 0.5 → **`4.5%`** (4.39 sits between 4.0 and 4.5, nearer 4.5).
- **Trailing Offset = 4.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.5 ÷ 5 = 0.90%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened** from 0.90% to 0.75%.
- Reason: AEV is a liquid blue-chip holding company with a deep book and narrow spread; the
  methodology calls for tightening the buffer toward the 0.25–0.5% zone for very liquid names.
  It is not quite top-tier (SM/BDO ≈ 0.25), so 0.75% is a measured tighten that still leaves a
  comfortable cushion rather than going to the floor.
- PSE tick check: at ₱32.35 the band is ₱20.00–₱49.98, one tick ≈ **₱0.05 (0.155%)**. A 0.75%
  buffer = ₱0.24 ≈ **4.85 ticks**, comfortably ≥ one tick + a typical 1–2 tick spread. Buffer ≥ 1 tick + spread? **Yes.**
- Rounding: `0.90% →` (tightened to 0.75) → round to nearest 0.25 → **`0.75%`**
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
