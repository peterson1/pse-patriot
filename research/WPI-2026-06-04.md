## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR%` → using the computed range, `2.0 × (1.2%…3.4%) = 2.4%…6.8%`.
- **Adjustments applied: large upward override for structural illiquidity + gap risk.**
- Reasons (each logged):
  - **Extreme thin liquidity.** Turnover is only ~₱50–100K/day on active days with frequent
    zero-volume sessions — among the thinnest names in the book. The methodology explicitly
    says widen for thin liquidity: a tight stop on such a name is picked off by a single noise
    print and fills poorly.
  - **Gappiness.** When WPI does trade it moves in multi-tick jumps: +10.8% (26 May), +6.5%
    (29 May), and a +6% intraday spike to ₱0.51 on 4 Jun that immediately faded back to ₱0.48.
    A stop must clear a typical ~5–6% adverse print plus gap cushion, so the offset has to sit
    well above the deflated ATR baseline.
  - **Large tick.** At ₱0.48 one PSE tick (₱0.005) is ~1.0% of price, so even "small" moves
    are ≥1% and a meaningful offset is needed to avoid tick-level whipsaw.
  - **Soft ceiling discipline.** The computed ATR does **not** support the ~12.5% ceiling (the
    stock is mostly flat, not in a high-volatility trend), so the offset sits in the upper-thin
    band rather than pinned at the cap.
- Rounding: landed at **9.0%** (nearest 0.5). This is ~1.3–3.8× the ATR baseline — the uplift
  is the liquidity/gap premium, not a quiet whipsaw-avoidance widen.
- **Trailing Offset = 9.0%**

Sanity check: a 9.0% trail off ₱0.48 first arms near ₱0.435 (₱0.48 × 0.91 ≈ ₱0.437 → ₱0.435 at
the ₱0.005 tick) — i.e. just above the ₱0.42 base the stock broke out of, a sensible "the pop
failed" level.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 9.0 ÷ 5 = 1.8%`
- **Adjustments applied: widen for extreme illiquidity / wide spread + large tick.**
- Reasons:
  - On a name this thin the bid–ask can be 1–2 ticks wide; when the stop triggers in a fast
    drop the limit must sit far enough below to actually fill. Methodology allows 2–3% for
    illiquid micro-caps.
  - The buffer must clear **1 tick + typical spread**. At ₱0.48, 1 tick = ₱0.005 = ~1.0%; a
    1–2 tick spread adds another ~1–2%. A 1.8% buffer (≈1.8 ticks) is too close to that
    threshold, so widen.
- PSE tick check: at ₱0.48 (the ₱0.2500–0.4950 band) one tick ≈ ₱0.005 (**~1.04%**), confirmed
  against the [PSE board-lot table](https://www.pinoymoneytalk.com/pse-board-lot-table-guide/)
  and corroborated by every observed WPI print being a multiple of ₱0.005. Chosen buffer
  2.5% = ₱0.012 ≈ 2.4 ticks ≥ 1 tick + spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.8% → 2.5%` (nearest 0.25; widened for illiquidity).
- **Limit Price = 2.5%**
