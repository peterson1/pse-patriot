## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 3.20% = 6.40%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen for thin liquidity + gappiness.**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  - **Liquidity:** CDC is deeply illiquid — typical daily volume ~30k–50k shares and several
    sessions under 20k (e.g. 4k on 3-Jun, 5k on 29-May, 7k on 19-May). Daily value turnover is
    roughly ₱2k–₱30k. Thin books gap and a stop placed near baseline risks being picked off by
    noise / filling poorly. Methodology directs nudging the offset up for low turnover.
  - **Gappiness / event risk:** the 19→20 May +9% jump on a 1.15M-share burst shows the name
    moves in discrete event-driven steps; a touch more room is warranted.
  - Widening = **+1.6 pts** above the 6.40% baseline → 8.0%. Stays well below the ~12.5% soft
    ceiling and above the ~2% floor.
- Rounding: `8.0% →` **`8.0%`** (already on a 0.5 grid)
- **Trailing Offset = 8.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 8.0 ÷ 5 = 1.60%`
- Adjustments applied: **widen for tick floor + illiquidity/spread.**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  - **PSE tick floor (binding):** at ₱0.58 the stock sits in the ₱0.50–₱4.99 board-lot band
    where the tick (minimum price increment) is **₱0.01 = 1.724% of price**. The 1.60%
    baseline buffer is ₱0.0093 — *less than a single tick* — so it would round to ₱0.00 and
    never fill. The buffer must be at least one tick.
  - **Spread / illiquidity:** on a name this thin the bid–ask spread is typically one tick
    (₱0.01) or wider. To clear **1 tick + 1-tick spread ≈ ₱0.02 (3.448%)** the buffer is set
    to ₱0.0203 ≈ **2 ticks**, ensuring the stop-limit still executes in a fast drop.
- PSE tick check: at ₱0.58, one tick = ₱0.01 (1.724%); chosen buffer 3.50% = ₱0.0203 ≈ 2.03
  ticks ⇒ buffer ≥ 1 tick + spread? **Yes** (₱0.0203 ≥ ₱0.02).
- Rounding: `3.448% → ` **`3.50%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 3.50%**
