## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.10% = 2.21%`
- Adjustments applied: none (sits at the soft floor)
- Reason: ATR% of ~1.1% is extremely low — typical of a steady office REIT with no gaps. The raw
  2.21% baseline already lands at the methodology's ~2% soft floor. Liquidity is moderate
  (~₱7M–₱21M/day) with a clean ₱0.02-tick book, so there is no thin-market/gappiness reason to
  widen above the floor. No reason to go below it either.
- Rounding: `2.21% → ` nearest 0.5 → **`2.0%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 2.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 2.0 ÷ 5 = 0.40%`
- Adjustments applied: none beyond rounding
- Reason: Liquidity is moderate and the book trades in tidy ₱0.02 ticks with a typical spread of
  about one tick, so no widening for thinness is warranted; nor is this a deep blue chip that
  would justify dropping to the 0.25 floor.
- PSE tick check: at ₱13.98 the price band is ₱10.00–₱19.98, so one tick = **₱0.02 (≈0.143%)**.
  Typical spread ≈ 1 tick (₱0.02). Required buffer ≥ 1 tick + spread ≈ ₱0.04 (≈0.29%).
  At 0.5% the buffer is ₱0.070 ≈ 3.5 ticks — comfortably clears tick + spread. **Yes.**
- Rounding: `0.40% → ` nearest 0.25 → **`0.5%`**
- **Limit Price = 0.5%**
