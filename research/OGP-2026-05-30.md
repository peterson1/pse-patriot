## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 3.30% = 6.60%`
- Adjustments applied: **none material** — liquidity is adequate and the ATR is reliable.
- Reasons considered (and why no override):
  - **Liquidity is fine, no widen.** ~₱25 M/day turnover and a ~₱85 B large-cap miner —
    solidly tradeable. Not blue-chip-deep (limited free float behind the parent), but well
    above the thin-name threshold that would justify padding the offset. No upward nudge.
  - **Gap/commodity risk already in the ATR.** OGP is gold-price sensitive and shows
    occasional heavy-volume reversal days (20, 28, 29 May), but those days are *inside* the
    14-day window and already lift the ATR (the 20 May TR of ₱2.25 and the two recent down
    days are captured). No need to add a separate gap premium on top.

    whipsaw; the stock is fading from near its ATH, and a tight trail is exactly what locks
    in the gains of the prior run.
- Rounding: `6.60% → ` **`6.5%`** (nearest 0.5; 6.60 rounds down cleanly, no distortion).
- **Trailing Offset = 6.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 6.5 ÷ 5 = 1.30%`
- Adjustments applied: **slight tighten toward the liquid end** (no widen).
- Reasons:
  - OGP's ~₱25 M/day turnover and ₱0.05 tick mean the book fills within a few ticks; an
    illiquidity widen is not warranted. The buffer can sit at the formula value, rounded
    toward the liquid side.
  - It is not a top-tier blue chip (SM/BDO sit at 0.25%), and it is a commodity name that can
    drop several ticks fast, so the buffer is kept comfortably above the spread rather than
    cut to the floor.
- PSE tick check: at ₱35.05 (the ₱20.00–₱49.95 band) one tick = **₱0.05 (≈0.143%)**,

- Rounding: `1.30% → ` **`1.25%`** (nearest 0.25).
