## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.75% = 5.49% ≈ 5.5%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** — the computed baseline is used as-is.
- Reasons considered (and why no override):
  - **Liquidity is deep → no widen.** ~₱79 M/day turnover and a ₱176 B large-cap utility:
    one of the more liquid names in the book. The methodology only widens the offset for
    *thin* names, so no upward nudge here.
  - **No gap premium needed.** Moves are orderly utility-style steps, not news gaps; the one
    wider pullback day is already inside the ATR window. No separate add-on.
  - **Volatility, not liquidity, sets the offset.** A liquid name does not get forced *below*
    its ATR baseline; 5.5% is simply 2× the current realized ATR%, and sits comfortably inside
    the ~2%–12.5% band.
- Rounding: `5.49% → ` **`5.5%`** (nearest 0.5).
- **Trailing Offset = 5.5%**

Sanity check: a 5.5% trail off ₱22.50 first arms near ₱21.25 (₱22.50 × 0.945 ≈ ₱21.26 → ₱21.25
at the ₱0.05 tick). That sits just below the ₱21.40–21.65 shelf the stock based at in
mid/late-April before the May run — a sensible "the advance has actually rolled over" level,
not ordinary pullback noise.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 5.5 ÷ 5 = 1.10%`
- Adjustments applied: **tighten one notch toward the liquid end.**
- Reasons:
  - MYNLD is a deep, ~₱79 M/day name; the book fills within a tick or two, so per the
    methodology ("tighten for very liquid blue chips") the buffer is brought below the formula
    value rather than padded.
  - It still must clear **1 tick + spread**: at ₱22.50, 1 tick = ₱0.05 (0.222%); a typical
    1–2 tick spread is ~0.22–0.44%. The chosen 0.75% = ₱0.169 ≈ **3.4 ticks** comfortably
    exceeds that, so the stop-limit still fills in a fast drop.
- PSE tick check: at ₱22.50 (the ₱20.00–₱49.95 band) one tick = **₱0.05 (≈0.222%)**,
  confirmed against the [PSE board-lot / tick table](https://www.pinoymoneytalk.com/pse-board-lot-table-guide/)
  and corroborated empirically — every observed MYNLD print (22.50, 22.30, 23.15, 23.80, …) is
  a multiple of ₱0.05. ✔
- Rounding: `1.10% → ` **`0.75%`** (nearest 0.25, after the liquidity tighten).
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
