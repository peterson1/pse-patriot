## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.79% = 5.58%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** — deep liquidity means no widen, and the ATR is genuine swing
  volatility (the ₱94→104 round-trip), so it is not trimmed toward the floor.
- Reasons:
  - **Deep blue-chip liquidity, no widen.** ~₱330–350 M/day turnover — the stop won't be picked off
    by spread noise, so the 2× baseline stands.
  - **Earnings event risk supports not tightening below baseline.** Q2 results are due around
    16/29-Jul; keeping the offset at the full computed 5.58% gives room for an earnings-driven swing.
- Rounding: `5.58% → ` **`5.5%`** (nearest 0.5).
- **Trailing Offset = 5.5%**

Sanity check: a 5.5% trail off a ₱103.00 high-water mark arms near ₱97.34 (₱103 × 0.945 → ₱0.10
tick → ₱97.30). That sits below the 1–14 Jul trading range (low ₱101.00) and just under the ₱97.5–99
consolidation of late June, so it fires on a real give-back of the early-July advance rather than on
routine ±1–3% chop.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 5.5 ÷ 5 = 1.10%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened to 0.75%** for deep liquidity — but **not** all the way to the
  0.5% prior/floor.
- Reasons:
  - BPI is one of the most liquid PSE banks (deep book, ~₱330–350 M/day), so a full 1.10% buffer is
    unnecessary — the book fills within a tick or two.
  - It is **not** cut back to 0.5% (the prior value), however, because (a) the offset is now wider and
    (b) Q2 earnings around 16/29-Jul can gap the stock through a thin limit; 0.75% adds fill cushion
    for that event while staying tight for a blue chip.
- PSE tick check: at ₱103.00 (the ₱100.00–₱199.90 band) one tick = **₱0.10 (≈0.097%)**; BPI prints in
  ₱0.10 steps above ₱100 (103.60, 105.90, 101.40, …), confirming the band. The 0.75% buffer (₱0.7725 ≈
  **7.7 ticks**) clears 1 tick + spread comfortably. ✔
- Rounding: `1.10% → ` **`0.75%`** (after liquidity tighten, nearest 0.25).
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
