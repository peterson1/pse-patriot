## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 0.045% = 0.09%`
- **Adjustments applied:** OVERRIDE the formula upward to a judgment value; below the ~12.5% soft ceiling, above the ~2% soft floor.
- **Reason for each adjustment:**
  - *ATR is non-informative (liquidity artifact).* The mechanical 0.09% would place the stop one tick below price and be picked off by any single odd-lot down-print. The methodology explicitly says: when there is no reliable ATR, do not fabricate — lean conservative and widen.
  - *Extreme illiquidity / gap risk.* FEU trades 10 shares on a typical day with many zero-trade sessions. On a near-empty book the price does not drift; it gaps in whole-peso steps (the one observed move was ₱5 = 0.63% in a single step on 10 shares). A stop must sit far enough below to survive a stray odd-lot print, yet a deep down-gap will blow straight through it regardless — so the offset is about avoiding *whipsaw on noise*, sized to clear several whole-peso steps.
  - *Not a speculative high-beta name.* Beta ≈ −0.08; this is a frozen blue-ish education stock, not a momentum small-cap, so it does not warrant the 12.5% ceiling. A moderate-wide offset fits.
- **Chosen value:** 6.0% (≈ ₱47.7 ≈ 95 ticks of room) — clears many whole-peso gap steps without being so wide it fails to protect.
- Rounding: judgment value already on the 0.5 grid → **6.0%**
- **Trailing Offset = 6.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 6.0 ÷ 5 = 1.2%`
- **Adjustments applied:** widen from 1.2% to 1.5% for extreme illiquidity / thin book.
- **Reason for each adjustment:** When the stop triggers on this name there may be very few resting bids; the limit must reach deep enough into the book to actually fill rather than chase a vanishing bid. Widening the buffer to 1.5% (≈ ₱11.9 ≈ 24 ticks) gives the exit a realistic chance of executing on a thin book. A blue-chip-style 0.25% would almost certainly not fill here.
- **PSE tick check:** at ₱795, the price band is ₱500.00–₱999.50 → one tick = **₱0.50** (0.063%). Buffer of 1.5% = ₱11.92 = ~24 ticks, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical (wide) spread. **Yes.**
- Rounding: 1.5% already on the 0.25 grid → **1.5%**
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
