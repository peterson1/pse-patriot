## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 0.85% = 1.71%` (below the ~2% soft floor)
- Adjustments applied: **+~2.3 pts for thin liquidity / thin-book gap & spread risk.**
- Reason: Turnover averages only ~₱230k–285k/day and is extremely lumpy — sessions as thin as
  1,000–32,000 shares. A pure ~1.7% stop on a book this thin is trivially picked off: the stock
  routinely oscillates ₱0.02–0.03 on negligible volume, and the largest recent True Range is
  ₱0.03 (≈1.7% at ₱1.76). The premium gives the trade room to survive thin-book noise while
  still exiting on a genuine breakdown. Sized to ~2.3 pts so the offset clears roughly 2× the
  worst recent gap plus a thin-book spread.
- Rounding: `~4.0% →` **`4.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 4.0%**

Sanity check vs. price structure: from the ₱1.760 high-water mark, a 4.0% offset triggers at
≈ ₱1.690 — just below the ₱1.700–1.730 June range floor and only a hair above the ₱1.680 print
the stock dipped to on 1-Jun (now outside the ATR window), so it still fires on a true break of
the base. The largest recent gap was ₱0.03; 4.0% gives ₱0.070 of room, ~2.3× that gap.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.0 ÷ 5 = 0.80%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 1.5%** for thin-book / wide-spread fill risk.
- Reason: On a name this illiquid the stop-limit must clear at least one tick plus the typical
  spread, or it may sit inside the spread and never fill in a fast drop. The 0.80% baseline
  (≈ ₱0.014 ≈ 1.4 ticks) is barely above a single tick — too thin for a book that prints as
  little as 1,000–32,000 shares/day. The limit buffer is the **critical protection** on this
  name and is held at the prior level despite the slightly tighter offset.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.760 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.568%) —
  confirmed by the data, which prints only in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 1.5% ≈ ₱0.0264 ≈
  **2.6 ticks**, comfortably exceeding 1 tick + a typical 1-tick thin-book spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.5% →` **`1.5%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
