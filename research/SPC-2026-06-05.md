## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.21% = 4.42%`
- Adjustments applied: small upward nudge for thin liquidity + intraday gappiness.
- Reason for each adjustment: turnover is only ~₱1.5M/day (thin/speculative tier), and the tape shows repeated intraday down-spikes to ~₱10.54 that recover by close. A stop set on close-to-close noise alone would be picked off by these wicks. The methodology's "nudge up for low turnover / gappiness" applies. The nudge is small because the close-to-close action is otherwise calm and the chosen posture is tight.
- Rounding: `4.42% → ` **`4.5%`** (nearest 0.5; the upward nudge and the rounding land at the same place).
- **Trailing Offset = 4.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.5 ÷ 5 = 0.9%`
- Adjustments applied: keep slightly wide (round up) for thin liquidity / wider effective spread.
- Reason for each adjustment: on a ~₱1.5M/day name the bid–ask can be 1–3 ticks (₱0.02–₱0.06) and prints are sparse; the limit must clear the spread comfortably so the exit actually fills in a fast drop. 0.9% rounds to 1.0% (nearest 0.25), and the slightly wider value is the prudent choice for a thin name.

- Rounding: `0.9% → ` **`1.0%`** (floor 0.25%, nearest 0.25).
