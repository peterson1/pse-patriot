## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 6.0 ÷ 5 = 1.2%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened** from 1.2% toward the blue-chip zone, but **not** all the
  way to the 0.25–0.5% deep-blue-chip floor.
- Reason: ICT's order book is deep (fills at a tick or two), which argues for a tight buffer;
  but current realized volatility is high (6% offset), so in a fast reversal a larger buffer
  protects the fill. 0.75% (₱5.85 at ₱780) is the balance.
- PSE tick check: at ₱780 the price sits in the ₱500.00–₱999.50 band → one tick = **₱0.50**
  (0.064%). Typical ICT spread is ~1–2 ticks (₱0.50–₱1.00, ~0.06–0.13%). Required buffer
  ≈ 1 tick + spread ≈ ₱1.50 (~0.19%). The chosen ₱5.85 (0.75%) comfortably clears this.
- Rounding: `0.75% →` **`0.75%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
