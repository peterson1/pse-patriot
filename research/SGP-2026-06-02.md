## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 3.93% = 7.86%`
- Adjustments applied: **none beyond baseline.**
  - Liquidity: adequate mid-cap turnover (≈₱60–120M/day) — no illiquidity widening needed.
  - Gappiness / event risk: high right now (new 52-wk highs, big intraday swings) — this
    argues *against* tightening the baseline; the tight 2.0× posture already gives ~2 ATR-days
    of room.
  - Soft bounds: 7.86% sits comfortably inside the ~2%–12.5% band.
- Rounding: `7.86% → ` **`8.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 8.0%**
- Sanity check: stop trigger ≈ ₱30.00 × (1 − 0.08) = **₱27.60**, just under the 27.65–28.50
  prior consolidation that is now support — a sensible placement.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 8.0 ÷ 5 = 1.6%`
- Adjustments applied: none (mid-cap liquidity is fine; gappiness keeps it from being trimmed).
- PSE tick check: at ₱30.00 the price sits in the ₱20.00–₱49.95 band → one tick = **₱0.05**
  (≈0.17%). Typical spread ~1–2 ticks (₱0.05–0.10 ≈ 0.17–0.33%). Buffer of 1.5% (₱0.45)
  clears 1 tick + spread by a wide margin — fills reliably in a fast drop.
- Rounding: `1.6% → ` **`1.5%`** (nearest 0.25; 1.6 is nearer 1.5 than 1.75)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%** (= 18.75% of the offset — squarely in the normal 15–20% band)
