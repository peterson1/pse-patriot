## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 5.02% = 10.04%`
- Adjustments applied: **none.**
- Reason: ACEN is a deeply liquid large-cap (~₱110M/day turnover), so the usual thin-liquidity
  *widening* does not apply, and there is no structural reason to *tighten* below the chosen
  2× posture. The elevated ATR is honest, current volatility — the stock has swung in a
  ₱2.90–3.53 band with 3–7% daily ranges. A tighter stop (e.g. 7% → trigger ₱3.07) would be
  whipsawed out by ordinary range action (it tagged ₱2.90 on 21-May and ₱2.95 on 26-May),
  whereas 10% (trigger ≈ ₱2.97 from a ₱3.30 high) lets the trade breathe within its range and
  fires on a genuine breakdown.
- Rounding: `10.04% →` **`10.0%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 10.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 10.0 ÷ 5 = 2.0%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened to 1.0%** for deep liquidity.
- Reason: ACEN is among the most actively traded PSE names; deep books fill at a tick or two,
  so the 2.0% formula buffer is unnecessarily wide. 1.0% still gives ample fill insurance in a
  fast drop while not leaving money on the table. (Not taken all the way to the 0.25% of the

- PSE tick check: at ₱3.300 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.303%) —
  confirmed by the data printing in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 1.0% ≈ ₱0.033 ≈ **3 ticks**,
  comfortably exceeding 1 tick + the typical 1-tick spread on a liquid name. ✔
- Rounding: `1.0% →` **`1.0%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.0%**
