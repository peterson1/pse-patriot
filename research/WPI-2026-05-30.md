**Caveat (critical): this ATR materially understates WPI's real risk.** Two artifacts deflate
it: (a) WPI did not trade meaningfully for ~2 weeks (14–25 May all flat at ₱0.42, several
zero-volume sessions), so most days contribute TR = 0; and (b) the cited series is rounded to
₱0.01, hiding sub-tick moves. A finer, **sparser** 3-decimal series (investing.com, which
skips no-print days) yields ATR ≈ ₱0.0158 → **ATR% ≈ 3.4%**. The honest read is therefore a
**range of ATR% ≈ 1.2%–3.4%**, and the raw 2× baseline (2.4%–6.8%) is *not* trustworthy on
its own for a name this illiquid — see §3.

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR%` → using the computed range, `2.0 × (1.2%…3.4%) = 2.4%…6.8%`.
- **Adjustments applied: large upward override for structural illiquidity + gap risk.**
- Reasons (each logged):
  - **Extreme thin liquidity.** Turnover is only ~₱50–100K/day with frequent zero-volume
    sessions — among the thinnest names in the book. The methodology explicitly says widen
    for thin liquidity: a tight stop on such a name is picked off by a single noise print and
    fills poorly.
  - **Gappiness.** When WPI does trade it moves in multi-tick jumps: +9.5% (26 May), +6.5%
    (29 May) up; −5.6% single-print drops seen (17 Apr, 4 May). A stop must clear a typical
    ~5–6% adverse print plus gap cushion, so the offset has to sit well above the deflated
    ATR baseline.
  - **Large tick.** At ₱0.49 one PSE tick (₱0.005) is ~1.0% of price, so even "small" moves
    are ≥1% and a meaningful offset is needed to avoid tick-level whipsaw.
  - **Soft ceiling discipline.** The prior entry sat at the 12.5% ceiling; the computed ATR
    does **not** support the maximum (the stock is mostly flat, not in a high-volatility
    trend), so the offset is brought down to the upper-thin band rather than pinned at the cap.
- Rounding: landed at **9.0%** (nearest 0.5). This is ~1.3–3.8× the ATR baseline — the uplift
  is the liquidity/gap premium, not a quiet whipsaw-avoidance widen.
- **Trailing Offset = 9.0%**

Sanity check: a 9.0% trail off ₱0.49 first arms near ₱0.445 (₱0.49 × 0.91 ≈ ₱0.446 → ₱0.445
at the ₱0.005 tick) — i.e. just above the ₱0.42–0.43 base the stock broke out of, a sensible
"the pop failed" level.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 9.0 ÷ 5 = 1.8%`
- **Adjustments applied: widen for extreme illiquidity / wide spread + large tick.**
- Reasons:
  - On a name this thin the bid–ask can be 1–2 ticks wide; when the stop triggers in a fast
    drop the limit must sit far enough below to actually fill. Methodology allows 2–3% for
    illiquid micro-caps.
  - The buffer must clear **1 tick + typical spread**. At ₱0.49, 1 tick = ₱0.005 = ~1.0%; a
    1–2 tick spread adds another ~1–2%. A 1.8% buffer (≈1.8 ticks) is too close to that
    threshold, so widen.
- PSE tick check: at ₱0.49 (the ₱0.2500–0.4950 band) one tick ≈ ₱0.005 (**~1.02%**),
  confirmed against the [PSE board-lot table](https://www.pinoymoneytalk.com/pse-board-lot-table-guide/)
  and corroborated by every observed WPI print being a multiple of ₱0.005. Chosen buffer
  2.5% = ₱0.01225 ≈ 2.45 ticks ≥ 1 tick + spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.8% → 2.5%` (nearest 0.25; widened for illiquidity).
- **Limit Price = 2.5%**

**Narrative:** Nothing structural about WPI has changed since 3-May-2026 — it remains an
extremely thin sub-peso micro-cap that sits flat then gaps. What changed is the **method**:
the prior entry defaulted a speculative micro-cap to the 12.5%/3.0% ceiling without computing
ATR, whereas this run derives the 14-day ATR from a contiguous OHLC series and finds realized
volatility is actually low (the stock spent 14–25 May glued to ₱0.42). The offset is therefore
driven by *liquidity/gap* judgment (≈9%) rather than the volatility ceiling, and the buffer is
re-scaled to that offset while still clearing the ₱0.005 tick + spread. Net: tighter, more
gain-protective values that remain illiquidity-aware. **Confidence: moderate-to-low** — ATR is
unstable for a name that barely trades; treat as provisional and revisit if WPI enters a
genuinely volatile/active phase.
