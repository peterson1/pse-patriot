## 2. 14-day ATR

**No published ATR was usable.** The only one found — Investing.com's technical page, ATR(14) = ₱0.2557, smoothing method undisclosed — was rejected on three grounds: (a) single-sourced with zero corroboration (TradingView served no ATR, Barchart 404s on `AT.PH`, Investagrams renders an empty template placeholder, PSE Edge and stockanalysis publish none); (b) the page demonstrably serves contradictory data — the same URL returned MA5 4.156 / RSI 57.4 in one fetch and MA5 9.482 / RSI 83.9 in another, and moving averages near ₱4.00 are impossible for a stock that traded ₱6.85–10.50 through June–July; (c) ₱0.2557 is ~40% below the value computed from a dual-verified OHLC series and sits below even the calmest available 14-session window, so it corresponds to no real window.

### The 20-Jul-2026 session — excluded from the table, but not ignored

The 20-Jul session was still marked "Open for trading" when captured, so it is **not** in the ATR table above. Its provisional bar is H 10.50 / L 9.05 / PrevClose 8.75, giving TR = |10.50 − 8.75| = **₱1.75** — over 4× the typical ~₱0.42, and by itself equal to a quarter of the entire 14-day sum.

Rolling that bar in (drop 30-Jun, add 20-Jul) gives sum ₱7.39 → ATR ₱0.5279 → **5.21%** of ₱10.14.

**Two distortions run in opposite directions, and both are logged rather than resolved silently:**

- An offset calibrated on the settled 30-Jun → 17-Jul window (**4.18%** of the live price) is **too tight** for the post-spike regime — the denominator grew 16% while the stock's peso volatility clearly did not shrink.
- Any ATR including 20-Jul (**5.21%**) is **inflated by a single unsettled outlier bar**.

**Blended ATR% used = (4.18 + 5.21) ÷ 2 = 4.70%**, with both bookends stated above so the human can re-derive either.

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 4.70% = 9.39%`
- Bookends for reference: settled-only `2.0 × 4.18% = 8.36%`; spike-inclusive `2.0 × 5.21% = 10.42%`
- Adjustments applied: **upward rounding only** — the raw 9.39% was rounded up rather than down to the nearest 0.5.
- Reason: three independent factors all push the same direction, and each is already partly reflected in the elevated ATR, so no further explicit widening on top of the blend was warranted — but none of them argues for rounding down:
  1. **Thin/speculative liquidity.** ≈₱13.1M/day turnover, with individual sessions under ₱3M (29-Jun: 247,100 shares; 22-Jun: 340,100; 01-Jul: 361,400 — roughly one-seventh of average). A stop triggered on a quiet session can fill materially away from the trigger.
  2. **Gaps are the dominant risk, not intraday travel.** 7 of 14 TRs were set by the gap term. 08-Jul opened above the prior close then reversed to close at its low (−6.25%); 09-Jul opened below the prior close; 20-Jul gapped +4.0% at the open. A stop tighter than ~4–5% would have been gapped through repeatedly in this window.
  3. **Elevated event risk.** Q2/H1-2026 results are due and unreleased (PSE 17-Q deadline ~14-Aug-2026; the prior-year analog landed 23-Jul), so a price-sensitive disclosure lands 3 days to ~4 weeks out. And the +15.89% spike on ~7× volume has **no identified catalyst** after exhaustive searching — the "firm copper prices" explanation was checked and **refuted** (copper was USD 6.25/lb on 20-Jul, +0.55% on the day and −1.60% over the trailing month, having hit a one-week low on 17-Jul). An unexplained idiosyncratic 16% move raises event risk rather than lowering it.
- Soft bounds check: 9.5% sits inside the ~2%–12.5% band, toward the speculative end — appropriate for a thin, gappy mining small-cap at a 52-week high. No floor or ceiling was hit.
- Rounding: `9.39% → ` **`9.5%`**
- **Trailing Offset = 9.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 9.5 ÷ 5 = 1.90%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 2.25%**
- Reason for each adjustment:
  1. **Spread is unverified — do not assume one tick.** No live or historical bid/ask for PSE:AT was retrievable by any agent or by the verifier. A single Investing.com snapshot of 10.14 / 10.16 (exactly 1 tick) could not be reproduced and is not relied on. Only the tick-implied *floor* is defensible; realistic spreads are plausibly 1–3 ticks and wider on thin days, but that is inference from turnover, not measurement. The buffer is sized so it does not depend on the optimistic case.
  2. **The tick regime just doubled.** The stock crossed ₱10.00 — from the ₱5.00–9.99 band (tick ₱0.010 ≈ 0.11% of price) into the ₱10.00–19.98 band (tick ₱0.02 ≈ 0.20%). Minimum achievable spread doubled in absolute terms with it.
  3. **Gap risk dominates.** With half the TRs gap-driven and a −25% June drawdown on the record, a stop triggered by a gap-down needs buffer that survives a fast move, not just the resting spread.
- PSE tick check: at ₱10.14, one tick ≈ ₱0.02 (0.197%). Buffer 2.25% = **₱0.228 ≈ 11.4 ticks** — comfortably ≥ 1 tick plus a 1–3 tick spread (₱0.02–0.06). ✅
- Ratio sanity check: 2.25 ÷ 9.5 = **23.7% of the offset**, slightly above the normal 15–20% band. That is the documented widening for an illiquid, wide-spread, gappy name and is logged deliberately, not by accident.
- Rounding: `1.90% → ` **`2.25%`**
- **Limit Price = 2.25%**

## 8. Known limitations (read before trusting these values)

1. **No catalyst identified for the 20-Jul +15.89% move.** Five research agents and an independent verifier all failed to find one, and the copper explanation was refuted. A 16% move on 7× volume to a fresh 52-week high is very unlikely to be newsless. **Before placing the order, open [PSE Edge `cmpy_id=34`](https://edge.pse.com.ph/companyPage/stockData.do?cmpy_id=34) in a browser and read the Disclosures tab for 17–20 Jul.** PSE Edge's disclosure *search* endpoint returns an unattributed exchange-wide feed (a "Declaration of Cash Dividends" and two press releases are timestamped 15:55 on 17-Jul, immediately before the gap — plausible but **not attributable** to AT from that feed), and the disclosure chart endpoint returns HTTP 415.
2. **The 20-Jul session is unsettled.** Its close is quoted 10.12 / 10.14 / 10.16 / 9.40 / 10.30 across sources (an ~8% spread explained by capture time, not source error). Every 20-Jul figure — OHLC, volume, turnover, the 52-week high — should be refetched after the official close.
3. **No verified bid-ask spread.** Only the tick-implied floor is defensible; the 2.25% buffer was sized deliberately not to depend on a 1-tick assumption.
4. **ATR is simple, not Wilder.** If your tooling expects Wilder smoothing, recompute — a Wilder update through 20-Jul gives ≈₱0.52.
5. **Tick table provenance.** Taken from a secondary reproduction, not a primary PSE document. PSE circular CN-2025-0046 proposes board-lot/tick amendments and was **not read**; if adopted, the tick figures may be stale.
6. **History depth.** No April or May 2026 sessions were retrieved, so the trend read is a ~6-week reconstruction. The early-June regime data is single-sourced to stockanalysis.com and is used as context only, never in the ATR.
7. Overall confidence: **medium**, driven almost entirely by limitation 1.
