## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.70% = 5.39%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** — I do not trim the spike out (see §2) and I do not widen further.
- Reasons:
  - **Blue-chip liquidity, no widen.** ~₱80–105 M/day turnover — a liquid PSEi telecom, no thin-name
    premium needed.
  - **Elevated volatility is partly a one-off but partly persistent.** The 29-Jun spike inflates the
    raw ATR, yet the recent ₱54–68 daily ranges (13–14 Jul) confirm the tape is genuinely wider than
    the calm ~₱35 baseline. Landing on the full-sample 5.39% balances the two.
- Rounding: `5.39% → ` **`5.5%`** (nearest 0.5).
- **Trailing Offset = 5.5%**

tick). That sits below the entire 25-Jun–14-Jul range (low ₱1,781 on 13-Jul), so it only fires on a
real break of the recent floor, not on the routine ₱30–68 intraday swings. (Ex-spike, a 4.5% offset
would arm near ₱1,715 — also below the range; the extra point of room is the cost of keeping the
