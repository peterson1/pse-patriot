## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.28% = 4.557%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** (no structural reason to deviate).
- Reason: MBT is a deep blue-chip bank with very high turnover (~₱229M/day, ~3.6–3.9M shares/day),
  so the realized ATR% is a clean, trustworthy read of normal daily noise — not liquidity artefact.
  No need to widen for thin liquidity, and the chosen tight posture argues against widening just to
  reduce whipsaw. The 2.0× baseline (4.56%) sits comfortably within the ~2%–12.5% bounds. The two
  gappy late-May sessions (22-May ₱66 spike, 25-May) are already captured in the ATR, so no extra
  event-risk premium is needed.
- Rounding: `4.557% → ` **`4.5%`** (nearest 0.5).
- **Trailing Offset = 4.5%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.5 ÷ 5 = 0.90%`
- Adjustments applied: **tightened for very liquid blue chip / deep book** (0.90% → 0.50%).
- Reason: MBT has one of the deepest order books among PSE banks (~₱229M/day). A deep book fills at
  a tick or two, so the full 0.90% buffer over-insures. Per the methodology's deep-blue-chip guidance
  (SM/BDO/SMPH sit at 0.25–0.50 despite the formula), 0.50% is ample fill insurance here. Kept at 0.50
  rather than dropping to 0.25 because the late-May/early-June sessions show MBT can drop fast (−2.7%
  on 3-Jun), so a slightly fatter buffer protects the stop-limit fill in a quick reversal.
- PSE tick check: at ₱63.80 the price band is ₱50.00–₱99.99, so one tick = **₱0.10** (≈0.157%).
  The 0.50% buffer = **₱0.32 ≈ 3 ticks**, comfortably ≥ one tick + a typical 1–2 tick spread on a
  deep large-cap. ✓
- Rounding: `0.90% (raw) → tightened to 0.50%` (nearest 0.25) **`0.50%`**
- **Limit Price = 0.5%**
