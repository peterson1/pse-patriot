## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 2.09% = 4.17%`
- Adjustments applied: **none**
- Reason for each adjustment: BPI is among the deepest books on the PSE (≈ ₱208M/day), so no
  thin-name or spread widening applies. There is no gappiness case either — all 14 daily ranges
  overlapped the prior close, so the ATR captures the full risk rather than understating overnight
  exposure. Baseline stands.
- Rounding: `4.17% → ` **`4.0%`** (4.17 is nearer 4.0 than 4.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 4.0%**

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.0 ÷ 5 = 0.8%`
- Adjustments applied: **none** (0.8% rounds down to the nearest 0.25 → 0.75%)
- Reason for each adjustment: deep liquidity means the buffer does not need widening, but it is
  deliberately **not** pushed to the 0.25% floor used for SM/BDO — BPI's daily range still runs
  ₱1.50–3.00, so a stop firing into a fast ₱2+ down-day needs meaningfully more than a tick of
  room below the trigger. The formula value already sits in the right place.
- PSE tick check: at ₱102.00 (₱100.00–₱199.90 band) one tick = **₱0.10 ≈ 0.098%**. Buffer 0.75% =
  ₱0.765 ≈ **7.7 ticks**, comfortably ≥ 1 tick + typical spread. ✔
- Rounding: `0.8% → ` **`0.75%`**
- **Limit Price = 0.75%**
