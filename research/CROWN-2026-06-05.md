### PSE disambiguation
The ticker **CROWN** on the PSE is **Crown Asia Chemicals Corporation**, a Philippine
manufacturer of plastic/synthetic resins, PVC pipes, PPR/HDPE and roofing products. It must
**not** be confused with the similarly named **Crown Equities, Inc.**, which trades on the PSE
under the *different* ticker **CEI** (≈₱0.077, a sub-centavo name) — a clear order-of-magnitude
separation that rules out any mix-up. Confirmed PSE-listed and **PHP-denominated**: quote
sources tag this name `PSE:CROWN` / `CROWN:PM` and price it in pesos (₱1.73–1.75), market cap
~₱1.06 billion. The ~₱1.7 sub-₱2 small-cap magnitude is consistent with a thin PSE micro-cap
and is far from any same-symbol foreign listing, so no wrong-exchange collision. Sources
cross-checked: stockanalysis.com (PSE:CROWN), investing.com PH, Bloomberg (CROWN:PM =
Philippines), pesobility.com (which separately lists CEI = Crown Equities, confirming the
two are distinct).

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.31% = 2.61%`
- Adjustments applied: **+~1.9 pts for thin liquidity / thin-book gap & spread risk.**
- Reason: Turnover averages only ~₱272k/day and is lumpy — several sessions under ₱30k (15k
  shares on 19-May, 27k on 21-May, just 11k on 1-Jun). A pure 2.6% stop on a book this thin is
  easily picked off: the stock routinely oscillates ₱0.02–0.04 on negligible volume and gapped
  down ₱0.03 (−1.75%) to ₱1.680 on 1-Jun before recovering to ₱1.750 by 5-Jun. The premium
  gives the trade room to survive that kind of thin-book noise/dip while still exiting on a
  genuine breakdown. The widening is held *moderate* because realized volatility is genuinely
  low and the name has been quietly trending up, not breaking down.
- Rounding: `~4.5% →` **`4.5%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 4.5%**

Sanity check vs. price structure: from the ₱1.750 high-water mark, a 4.5% offset triggers at
≈ ₱1.671 — comfortably above the ₱1.640–1.650 range-floor cluster (so it still arms and fires
on a true break of the base) yet below the ₱1.680 level the stock dipped to on 1-Jun (so a
repeat of that thin-book dip would *not* whipsaw the trade out). For reference, the largest
recent gap was ₱0.03; 4.5% gives ₱0.079 of room, ~2.6× that gap.

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.5 ÷ 5 = 0.9%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 1.5%** for thin-book / wide-spread fill risk.
- Reason: On a name this illiquid the stop-limit must clear at least one tick plus the typical
  spread, or it may sit inside the spread and never fill in a fast drop. The 0.9% baseline
  (≈ ₱0.0158 ≈ 1.6 ticks) is barely above a single tick — too thin for a book that prints as
  little as 11k shares/day. The limit buffer is the **critical protection** on this name, so it
  is held at the prior 1.5% rather than tightened with the offset.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.750 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.571%) —
  empirically confirmed by the data, which prints only in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 1.5% ≈ ₱0.0263
  ≈ **2.6 ticks**, comfortably exceeding 1 tick + a typical 1-tick thin-book spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.5% →` **`1.5%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
