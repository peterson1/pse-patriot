manufacturer of PVC/PPR/HDPE pipes and synthetic resins. It is **not** **Crown Equities, Inc.**,
which trades on the PSE under the *different* ticker **CEI** (a sub-centavo name) — an
order-of-magnitude separation that rules out any mix-up. Confirmed PSE-listed and
**PHP-denominated**: stockanalysis.com tags it `PSE:CROWN` and investing.com PH quotes it in
pesos (₱1.75–1.76), market cap ~₱1.07B. The sub-₱2 small-cap magnitude is far from any
same-symbol foreign listing. The two sources agree to the cent on the 11-Jun and 15-Jun
closes, and every overlapping row matches the prior 5-Jun note, validating the series.

## 3. Trailing Offset computation

- Baseline (tight posture): `2.0 × ATR% = 2.0 × 1.22% = 2.44%`
- Adjustments applied: **+~2.0 pts for thin liquidity / thin-book gap & spread risk.**
- Reason: Turnover averages only ~₱274k/day and is extremely lumpy — sessions as thin as 1,000
  shares (9-Jun) and 11,000–12,000 shares (1-Jun, 15-Jun). A pure 2.4% stop on a book this thin
  is easily picked off: the stock routinely oscillates ₱0.02–0.06 on negligible volume and
  gapped down ₱0.03 (−1.75%) to ₱1.680 on 1-Jun. The premium gives the trade room to survive
  thin-book noise while still exiting on a genuine breakdown. Held *moderate* because realized
  volatility is genuinely low and the name has been quietly trending up, not breaking down.
- Rounding: `~4.4% →` **`4.5%`** (nearest 0.5)
- **Trailing Offset = 4.5%**

Sanity check vs. price structure: from the ₱1.760 high-water mark, a 4.5% offset triggers at
≈ ₱1.681 — just at the ₱1.680 level the stock dipped to on 1-Jun and above the ₱1.640–1.650
range-floor (so it still fires on a true break of the base). The largest recent gap was ₱0.03;
4.5% gives ₱0.079 of room, ~2.6× that gap. (Note: as the high-water mark has risen ₱1.75 →
₱1.76, the same 4.5% now sits marginally closer to the ₱1.68 thin-book dip than in the 5-Jun

## 4. Limit Price (execution buffer) computation

- Baseline: `Trailing Offset ÷ 5 = 4.5 ÷ 5 = 0.9%`
- Adjustments applied: **widened to 1.5%** for thin-book / wide-spread fill risk.
- Reason: On a name this illiquid the stop-limit must clear at least one tick plus the typical
  spread, or it may sit inside the spread and never fill in a fast drop. The 0.9% baseline
  (≈ ₱0.0158 ≈ 1.6 ticks) is barely above a single tick — too thin for a book that prints as
  little as 1,000 shares/day. The limit buffer is the **critical protection** on this name.
- PSE tick check: at ₱1.760 the price sits in the ₱1.00–4.99 band, tick = **₱0.01** (0.568%) —
  confirmed by the data, which prints only in ₱0.01 steps. Buffer of 1.5% ≈ ₱0.0264 ≈
  **2.6 ticks**, comfortably exceeding 1 tick + a typical 1-tick thin-book spread. ✔
- Rounding: `1.5% →` **`1.5%`** (nearest 0.25)
- **Limit Price = 1.5%**
